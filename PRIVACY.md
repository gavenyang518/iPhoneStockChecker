# Privacy policy – RestockCheck Chrome extension

Last updated: 2026-11-15

The RestockCheck extension requires no account and contains no third-party analytics or ads. Stock checks run in your browser, and settings are stored locally on your device.

- **Direct Apple stock checks:** Stock pickup checks are sent from your browser directly to Apple endpoints (`www.apple.com`, `www.apple.com.cn`). Apple receives the store and part numbers you are querying as part of normal web requests.
- **Model catalog & store search:** Model lists and Apple Store directories are retrieved from public endpoints on `restockcheck.com` (`/api/stock/catalog`, `/api/stock/stores`). When you type a city or ZIP code to search for nearby stores, that query string is sent to `/api/stock/stores` to return matching store locations.
- **Upgrade link:** ONLY the 'Upgrade' link carries your selected watch configuration (country, family, color, storage, store) and campaign attribution parameters (`utm_source=chrome_extension&utm_medium=extension&utm_campaign=upgrade`). Header and footer links to restockcheck.com are plain links without configuration or attribution parameters.
- **Local device storage:** Your watch list, check interval, and sound toggle are stored locally in `chrome.storage.local` on your device. They are never synchronized to our servers and are deleted when you remove the extension.
- **No third-party analytics or ads:** The extension contains no third-party analytics or advertising scripts.
- **Contact:** If you have questions about privacy, visit https://restockcheck.com/contact or email support@restockcheck.com.
